NSAIDs

Pharmacological Class 12 Results

Nonsteroidal Anti-inflammatory Drugs

Agents used primarily to treat inflammation, mild-to-moderate pain, and fever. Specific uses include the treatment of headaches, arthritis, sports injuries, and menstrual cramps.

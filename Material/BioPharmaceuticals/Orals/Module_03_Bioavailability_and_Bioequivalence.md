# Module 03: Bioavailability (F) and Bioequivalence

## Absolute Bioavailability ($F$)
The fraction of an oral dose that reaches systemic circulation compared to an IV dose.
$$ F = \frac{AUC_{oral} \cdot Dose_{IV}}{AUC_{IV} \cdot Dose_{oral}} $$

## Relative Bioavailability
Comparison between two non-IV formulations (e.g., Tablet vs. Capsule).
$$ F_{rel} = \frac{AUC_{A} \cdot Dose_{B}}{AUC_{B} \cdot Dose_{A}} $$

## Bioequivalence
Two products are bioequivalent if their $F$, $C_{max}$, and $T_{max}$ are not significantly different (usually within 80-125% range).

## Factors Influencing Bioavailability
1. **First-Pass Metabolism**: Breakdown in liver/gut wall before reaching blood.
2. **Solubility**: Drug must be in solution to be absorbed.
3. **Stability**: Degradation in stomach acid.
4. **Food Effects**: Can increase or decrease absorption.

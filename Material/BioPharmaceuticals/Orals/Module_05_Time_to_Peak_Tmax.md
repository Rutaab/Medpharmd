# Module 05: Time to Peak ($T_{max}$)

## Definition
$T_{max}$ is the time at which the peak plasma concentration ($C_{max}$) occurs. It is an indicator of the **rate** of absorption.

## Formula
$$ T_{max} = \frac{\ln(k_a / k_e)}{k_a - k_e} $$

## Key Characteristics
- **Independent of Dose**: $T_{max}$ does not change with the dose in first-order kinetics.
- **Independent of $F$**: $T_{max}$ does not depend on how much drug is absorbed, only how fast.
- **Relation to Rates**:
  - If $k_a$ increases, $T_{max}$ decreases (faster peak).
  - If $k_e$ increases, $T_{max}$ decreases.

## Clinical Use
- Measures the **onset of action**.
- Used in bioequivalence studies to compare absorption rates between generic and brand-name drugs.

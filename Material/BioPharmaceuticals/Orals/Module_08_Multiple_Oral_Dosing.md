# Module 08: Multiple Oral Dosing

## Concept
Similar to IV bolus, repeated oral dosing leads to accumulation until steady state is reached. However, the fluctuations between peak and trough are influenced by $k_a$.

## Average Steady-State Concentration ($C_{av,ss}$)
$$ C_{av,ss} = \frac{F \cdot Dose}{Cl \cdot \tau} $$
*Note: This formula is identical to the IV formula except for the inclusion of bioavailability ($F$).*

## Fluctuations
- Faster absorption ($high\ k_a$) leads to higher peaks and lower troughs (greater fluctuation).
- Slower absorption (e.g., controlled release) leads to "blunted" peaks and higher troughs (less fluctuation).

## Clinical Goal
Maintain $C_{av,ss}$ within the therapeutic range while ensuring $C_{max,ss} < MTC$ and $C_{min,ss} > MEC$.

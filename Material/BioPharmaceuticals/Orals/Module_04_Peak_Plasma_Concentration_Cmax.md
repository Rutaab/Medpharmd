# Module 04: Peak Plasma Concentration ($C_{max}$)

## Definition
$C_{max}$ is the maximum drug concentration achieved in the plasma after oral administration.

## Formula
$$ C_{max} = \frac{F \cdot Dose}{V_d} \cdot e^{-k_e T_{max}} $$

## Factors Affecting $C_{max}$
- **Dose**: $C_{max}$ is directly proportional to dose (if linear).
- **$k_a$**: Increasing $k_a$ (faster absorption) increases $C_{max}$.
- **$k_e$**: Increasing $k_e$ (faster elimination) decreases $C_{max}$.
- **$V_d$**: Increasing $V_d$ decreases $C_{max}$.

## Clinical Significance
- Must stay below the **Minimum Toxic Concentration (MTC)**.
- Must be above the **Minimum Effective Concentration (MEC)** for efficacy.
- Important for drugs requiring a fast onset of action (e.g., analgesics).

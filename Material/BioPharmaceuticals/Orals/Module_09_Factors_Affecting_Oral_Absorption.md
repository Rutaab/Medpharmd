# Module 09: Factors Affecting Oral Absorption

## 1. Physicochemical Factors
- **pKa and pH**: According to the pH-partition hypothesis, only unionized drug is absorbed.
- **Particle Size**: Smaller particles = higher surface area = faster dissolution.
- **Salt Form**: Often more soluble than the free acid/base.

## 2. Physiological Factors
- **Gastric Emptying Time**: Slower emptying can delay $T_{max}$.
- **Intestinal Motility**: High motility may decrease absorption time.
- **Surface Area**: The small intestine is the primary site of absorption due to villi/microvilli.

## 3. Formulation Factors
- **Excipients**: Fillers, binders, and lubricants can affect disintegration and dissolution.
- **Coatings**: Enteric coatings prevent release in the stomach.

## 4. Patient Factors
- **Age**: Changes in gastric pH and blood flow.
- **Disease**: Malabsorption syndromes, Crohn's disease.
- **Interactions**: Food-drug or drug-drug interactions (e.g., antacids with tetracycline).
